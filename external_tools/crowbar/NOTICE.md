# Crowbar command-line decompiler

`cli/CrowbarCommandLineDecomp.exe` is an unmodified local copy of the executable
supplied with the earlier `gmod-to-mmd-porter` project. Its SHA-256 is
`4b5fc8f5092448c1f8fe12f6849bf8ee3996406f02109ec90ab800c6cf145b2a`.

The command-line fork is by UltraTechX, based on Crowbar by ZeqMacaw, with
compilation fixes credited to Rainyan. This bundled fork reports Crowbar 0.68.

it as a separate process and isolates its startup preferences in per-run
scratch storage.
